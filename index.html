A documented chapter

A record of work, voyages and change

Rassvet was my first yacht. Aboard her I learned to sail, maintain a boat and make decisions at sea. This site preserves what we did together.

01

Proven on passage

Four cruising seasons across Sweden, Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands and Norway, including night passages, fog and fresh winds.

02

Continuously maintained

Hull, deck, engine, rig, sails and onboard systems were improved throughout Andrei's ownership, with the work recorded year by year.

03

Three names, four ownership chapters

FULLSTAR was based in Gothenburg from 1975 to 2021, MONTEZUMA in Kalmar from 2021 to 2023, and RASSVET in Kiel from 2023 to 2026. Her handover to Janne and Rabea begins the next chapter.

04

The story continues

In August 2026, Rassvet passed to Janne and Rabea — a capable, hardworking couple surrounded by friends who know how to build, repair and care for things well.

A new chapter · August 2026

Fair winds, Janne and Rabea

Rassvet has passed to a kind and hardworking couple with many friends who are skilled craftspeople. I believe she will grow more beautiful with the years and may one day even surpass her prime.

Her name

The dawn of my path into sailing

When I named her RASSVET — “dawn” in Russian — that was the meaning I gave it. She became exactly that: the beginning of my life in sailing.

What remains

More than miles in a logbook

I am grateful for the time I owned this hull, for everything she taught me, for the way she influenced and changed me, and for all the people I met because of her.

What comes next

A boat with people around her

I wish Janne and Rabea many wonderful moments aboard this beautiful yacht, and Rassvet many more years of sailing, care and new stories.

Maintenance history

Four years of documented maintenance and upgrades

The record below covers Andrei's ownership from 2023 to 2026, including professional yard work, equipment upgrades, winter storage and annual underwater-hull maintenance.

2026

Rigging, energy system and structural improvements

  • Rigging: new upper shrouds and backstay, complete with new turnbuckles; new shroud retainers at the spreader tips; new turnbuckle covers.
  • Engine and drivetrain: raw-water pump impeller replaced; new shaft seal installed; full engine service carried out by Davids Werft.
  • Solar and electrical: two new 100 W solar panels and a new 12 V automotive socket in the forecabin.
  • Bow and anchoring: new chain pipe into the forepeak for the anchor rode, with a custom marine-plywood box inside the forepeak; new bow platform.
  • Cockpit and deck: removable cockpit sole section added for access to the propeller shaft; stainless-steel hatch hinges replaced.
  • Exterior woodwork: all exterior wooden components reinforced with epoxy and finished with three coats of Epifanes varnish; the tiller received five coats.
  • Underwater hull: old antifouling sanded and renewed with two coats of Hempel Mille NCT; propeller coated with Hempel Silic One.
  • Interior: new mirror installed.
2025

Major hull, deck and sail-handling refit

  • Running rigging: new main halyard and dedicated mast block; new genoa sheets.
  • Hull finish: hull repainted in her distinctive yellow colour using Epifanes Mono-Urethane.
  • Deck finish: deck repainted white using Epifanes Mono-Urethane and a new KiwiGrip non-slip coating applied.
  • Engine: full engine service carried out by Davids Werft.
  • Plumbing: new mixer tap and hand-held bidet spray installed.
  • Winter storage: yacht stored indoors in a hangar.
  • Underwater hull: old antifouling sanded and renewed with two coats of Hempel Mille NCT; propeller coated with Hempel Silic One.
2024

Navigation, deck equipment and keel maintenance

  • Engine and cooling: full engine service carried out by Davids Werft; fuel pre-filter and raw-water strainer installed.
  • Navigation instruments: new Silva 100 compass, Windex and NASA Marine Clipper Wind, Depth and Log instruments.
  • Visibility and lighting: new radar reflectors and LED lighting installed inside and outside.
  • Deck hardware: winches stripped, serviced and lubricated; Barton Winchers fitted; new rail-mounted anchor holder installed.
  • Sails and canvas: genoa seams renewed; sprayhood clear window and seams renewed.
  • Sealing: locker and hatch seals renewed.
  • Keel: keel bolts and the keel-to-hull joint professionally serviced by Davids Werft.
  • Winter storage and underwater hull: yacht stored indoors in a hangar; propeller coated with Hempel Silic One.
2023

Purchase and electrical-system upgrade

  • Rassvet purchased by Andrei in Sweden and sailed to Germany.
  • Charging: Victron Energy 15 A MPPT solar charge controller and Victron 230 V / 30 A shore-power charger installed.
  • Electrical protection: new 16 A circuit breakers installed.
  • Batteries: two new 105 Ah AGM batteries installed.
  • Bilge system: new bilge pump installed.
  • Underwater hull: the previous owner renewed the epoxy barrier coating before the 2023 purchase.

On board in 2026

Systems and sail wardrobe at handover

A snapshot of the principal equipment and fixed systems aboard Rassvet at the end of the 2023–2026 chapter.

Propulsion

Nanni Diesel N2.14

Installed in 2005 with fewer than 750 engine hours. Professionally serviced by Davids Werft in 2024, 2025 and 2026. The engine starts reliably on the first attempt.

Sail wardrobe

Three sails for coastal cruising

Mainsail with two reefing points, 140% furling genoa and a spinnaker used only a few times and remaining in near-new condition.

Energy system

Solar and shore-power charging

Two 100 W solar panels, Victron Energy 15 A MPPT controller, Victron 230 V / 30 A charger and two 105 Ah AGM batteries.

Fixed onboard systems

Navigation, heating and utilities

NASA Marine Clipper Wind, Depth and Log instruments, Silva 100 compass, Wallas diesel heater, LED lighting and electric bilge pump.
